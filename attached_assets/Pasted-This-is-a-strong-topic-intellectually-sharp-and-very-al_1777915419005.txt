🧠 Event Description (polished + compelling)

From Human in the Model to Human in the Loop: The Silencing of the Human Voice in the Training of Foundational AI Models

As frontier AI systems exhaust the supply of human-generated data, labs are increasingly turning inward—training models on synthetic outputs generated by other models.

But what happens when intelligence begins learning from itself?

This session explores a critical inflection point:

* The loss of organic human variance in training data
* The rise of self-referential model ecosystems
* The risk of flattened perception, creativity, and meaning
* And the deeper question: Can AI truly understand humanity without humanity embedded in its foundation?

As humans shift from being the source of intelligence to merely evaluators of it, we must ask:

👉 Are we advancing intelligence… or slowly removing the very signal that makes it meaningful?
